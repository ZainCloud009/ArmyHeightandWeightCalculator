Privacy Policy

Your personal body measurements are processed strictly in your browser and never collected.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Client-Side Processing

All calculations performed on Army Height and Weight Calculator (including height, waist circumference, neck, hip, weight, and fitness test scores) run entirely on your local device via client-side JavaScript. We do not transmit, log, or store your biometric data on any external server.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require user accounts, logins, military identification, DOD ID numbers, social security numbers, unit affiliations, or any personally identifiable military information.

3. Web Analytics & Cookies

Like most modern websites, we may utilize standard aggregated analytical cookies to monitor anonymized site traffic, load speeds, and error rates to improve site reliability. You can disable cookies at any time via your browser settings.

4. Contact Inquiries

If you contact us via our contact form or support email, your name and email address will be used solely to respond to your specific question and will never be sold or provided to marketing lists.
