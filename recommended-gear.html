What Does AR 600-9 Require for Measuring Tapes?

According to AR 600-9 Appendix B, measuring tapes used for military body composition assessments must meet strict technical specifications:

  • Material: The tape must be made of non-stretchable material, preferably fiberglass. Cloth or tailor tapes that stretch under tension are strictly prohibited.
  • Width: The tape width must be between 1/4 inch and 3/8 inch for optimal alignment.
  • Calibration: Calibrated in inches and tenths of an inch (0.1 in) or 1/4 inch increments.
  • Retraction: Retractable spring-loaded casing with self-locking mechanisms is authorized to ensure consistent tape tension without skin depression.
